Peaceful Lake Loop

Published on: October 27, 2023

Difficulty: Easy

Length: 3 miles

A gentle loop around the serene Peaceful Lake. This trail is perfect for families and casual walkers. Enjoy the beautiful reflections on the water and the surrounding forest.

Features

  • Lakeside walk
  • Flat terrain
  • Picnic spots
