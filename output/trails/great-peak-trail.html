Great Peak Trail

Published on: October 26, 2023

Difficulty: Challenging

Length: 10 miles

This trail leads to the summit of Great Peak, offering breathtaking panoramic views. The path is well-maintained but steep in sections, requiring good fitness.

Highlights

  • Summit views
  • Alpine meadows
  • Wildlife spotting opportunities
