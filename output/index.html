Peaceful Lake Loop

27 October 2023

A gentle loop around the serene Peaceful Lake. This trail is perfect for families and casual walkers. Enjoy the beautiful reflections on the water and the surrounding forest.

Great Peak Trail

26 October 2023

This trail leads to the summit of Great Peak, offering breathtaking panoramic views. The path is well-maintained but steep in sections, requiring good fitness.

Highlights

  • Summit views
  • Alpine meadows
  • Wildlife spotting opportunities
